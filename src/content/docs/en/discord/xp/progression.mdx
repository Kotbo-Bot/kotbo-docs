## Your rank card

<DiscordCommand
  command="/rank"
  botResponse="*The card is sent as an image: username, title, badges, level, XP bar and rank on the server.*"
/>

The card shows the level, the XP of the current tier, the rank on the server and, where set, the badges, title, frame and background you picked.

:::tip[It is customised from the dashboard]
Background, pattern, frame, XP bar, font, emojis and badges are set under **My profile**, in the *My rank card* section. The achievements that unlock titles and frames are detailed in the [Leveling & XP](/en/dashboard/xp/leveling/) guide. The card follows its owner from server to server, but the level and rank shown stay specific to the server you are on.
:::

`/profile` opens the community profile, with the [Daily Algo](/en/discord/communaute/daily-algo/) progression. `/profil` - no trailing "e" - opens the detailed staff profile: you must be listed in the [staff directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/) to use it, and so must the person you look up. Without the **utilisateur** option it opens your own file, and its reply is visible to you alone.

## Leaderboards

* `/leaderboard`: Server activity: **Messages**, **Voice**, **Mixed** or **XP / Levels**.
* `/top`: The game: **Money**, **RPG** (level and XP), **Items**, **Monsters** slain, **Fishing**.
* `/prestige`: Prestige points, their tiers and the streaks in progress.
* `/richest`: The richest members, a shortcut to the money ranking.

`/leaderboard` takes three settings: the **type**, the **style** - *Image (Modern)* or *Text (V2)* - and the **period**, 7, 30 or 90 days. The period is ignored for the XP ranking, which is global. One more option refreshes the leaderboard **every hour** in the channel where it was posted.

## Reputation

Reputation does not reward the amount of activity, but how useful others found someone.

<DiscordCommand
  command="/rep give membre:@Camille raison:Helped out in the support channel"
  embedTitle="Reputation given"
  embedDescription="Alex gave a +rep to Camille\n\nCamille total reputation: 47"
/>

* `/rep give`: Give a +rep to a member.
* `/rep check`: View a member's reputation.
* `/rep top`: The reputation leaderboard.

:::caution[Three votes a day]
The count is kept in the server's time zone. Past that, the vote is refused. Nobody can vote for themselves, nor for a bot.
:::

## The profile widget

`/widget` manages what Kotbo publishes about you on Discord's social layer: `activer` puts it on your profile for this server, `desactiver` removes it, and `refresh` updates its data by hand.

## Server quests

`/quests` lists the open quests, your progress and each deadline. **Rewards pay out on their own** as soon as the target is reached: there is nothing to claim, and the XP earned goes through the leveling system, level-up and reward roles included.
