## The four areas to know

1. **The active server**, in the top bar. Click it to switch servers.
2. **The sidebar**, grouped by purpose: General, Moderation, Security, XP system, Economy & RPG, Community, Staff, Cross-server and Configuration. Right at the bottom, outside those groups, sit **Administration** (reserved for the instance administrators), [**Billing**](../../dashboard/configuration/facturation/) and your profile.
3. **The main area**, which shows the page you picked and its actions.
4. **Your profile**, in the top right, to replay the tutorial, open your settings or sign out.

## The overview

The home page brings together everything your team needs at a glance: members, online activity, voice channel usage, growth and recent events.

The **Edit layout** button lets you move, hide or resize the blocks. Your layout is personal: it does not change what the rest of the staff sees.

:::caution[Unsaved changes]
On configuration pages, a bar appears at the bottom of the screen as soon as a setting changes. Click **Save** before moving to another page. Kotbo warns you if you try to leave with pending changes.
:::

## Quick navigation

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="kbd">/</span> | Search the sidebar |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">K</span> | Search the sidebar (twice: command palette) |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">G</span> | Switch server |
| <span class="kbd">G</span> then <span class="kbd">D</span>, or <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">D</span> | Open the overview |
| <span class="kbd">G</span> then <span class="kbd">M</span>, or <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">M</span> | Open members |
| <span class="kbd">G</span> then <span class="kbd">C</span>, or <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">C</span> | Open the Management center |
| <span class="kbd">G</span> then <span class="kbd">L</span>, or <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">L</span> | View the activity logs |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">N</span> | Show every shortcut |

Shortcuts do not fire while you are typing in a field or a text area.

:::tip[The command palette knows the tabs too]
A page such as **Economy & RPG** or **Analytics** splits its content into tabs. The palette knows them: searching for "bestiary" or "retention" takes you straight to the right tab, without going through the page first.

It only offers what your role opens: pages of a disabled module and pages closed to you never show up there, exactly as in the sidebar. A change to the role access is picked up without waiting or reloading the page.
:::

## The emoji picker

Everywhere a setting asks for an emoji - reactions, ticket panels, rules, shop, welcome messages - the picker offers three sources:

* **Emojis**: the standard Unicode emojis.
* **Server**: your server's custom emojis, with the number of slots used.
* **Upload**: create an emoji directly on the server from an image (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WEBP), giving it a name.

:::note[Uploading needs a permission]
*The bot lacks the "Manage Expressions" permission on this server, or you are not an administrator.* The Server tab shows *Server emojis unavailable* when Kotbo cannot read them.
:::

## Badges and page states

- **Beta**: the feature works but may still change. You can report a problem from the banner on the page.
- **WIP**: the feature is not available yet, or needs specific activation.
- **Module disabled**: the page can disappear from the navigation until the module is enabled.

Carry on with [enabling your modules](../modules/).
