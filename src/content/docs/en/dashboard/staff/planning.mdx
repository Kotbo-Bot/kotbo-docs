<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Planning</strong> gathers meetings, Discord calls, absences and tasks for your team on a single agenda.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Staff → Schedule** section: [Open the planning ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/planning).
You must be registered as a staff member to create anything here.
:::

:::note[Times follow the server's zone]
Every date entered on this page - absences, calls, tasks - is read in the **server's time zone**, recalled below the fields as *Time interpreted in …* and set from the [dashboard home](/en/dashboard/general/accueil/).
:::

---

## Reading the agenda

Four calendars overlay each other, each toggled separately under **My calendars**:

| Calendar | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Meetings** | Team check-ins from the [Meetings](/en/dashboard/staff/reunions/) module. |
| **Discord calls** | Scheduled voice calls. |
| **Absences** | Declared unavailability. |
| **Tasks** | Assigned tasks. |

The **People** panel filters the agenda on one or more members, with **None** and **All** shortcuts.

The **My day** view shows your deadlines for today and, when there is nothing, the message *Your day is free!*.

### Absence notifications

The alert configuration can enable a **Discord relay**: a message is sent to a chosen **alerts channel**, with a **role to mention**, and optionally through a **Discord webhook URL**.

## Scheduling a call

A call creates a voice channel or a stage at a given time.

* **Temporary channel** or **Existing channel**.
* **Voice** or **Stage** type.
* **Auto-delete when empty**: cleans up the temporary channel after the call.
* **Guests - Staff** and **Guests - Server members**: the search covers the server's members.

### Who can create calls

The **Call permissions** button opens the setting:

* **Everyone**: all members with access to the staff dashboard.
* **Specific roles / members**: a list of **allowed roles** and **allowed members**.

## Tasks and reminders

A **task** carries a title, a description, a **priority** (*low*, *medium*, *high*) and an assignee. Tasks marked **Important** move to the top.

On any agenda item, the **Reminders** block schedules a notification:

* a **date and time**;
* a **delivery channel**: *Direct Message* or *This Discord channel*;
* a free-text **note**, for example *Meeting in 5 min*.

Each reminder then shows whether it is *(Scheduled)* or *(Sent)*, and can be deleted.
